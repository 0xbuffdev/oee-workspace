# Progress and evidence log

Use this file to record course progress, project work, and links to demonstrable evidence. It is a working record, not a substitute for the completion requirements defined by OEE.

Keep this file in the root of your personal or group workspace. Record evidence links as work is completed.

Use a status that accurately describes your progress, such as `not started`, `in progress`, `blocked`, or `complete`. These are personal tracking values; they are not OEE's formal course status labels.

Mark a course complete only when all its required completion conditions have been met and the evidence is linked.

Do not begin a gated project until its stated prerequisites have been met. A project is not complete merely because its code exists; its required tests, evaluation, documentation, and retrospective must also be completed.

## Evidence conventions

For each completed course or project, link directly to the relevant artifacts, commits, reports, test results, or demonstrations.

Evidence should make it possible to determine:

- What was built, measured, tested, or explained.
- How the result can be reproduced.
- Which requirements the evidence satisfies.
- What assumptions and limitations remain.
- What was learned from a failure, correction, or unexpected result.

A checkbox without supporting evidence is not sufficient.

## Record integrity

- Keep entries factual and up to date.
- Link to the relevant artifact rather than relying on a verbal claim.
- Record blockers and incomplete work honestly.
- Do not publish credentials, private learner information, or sensitive evidence.
- Revise a completion entry if its supporting evidence is later found to be incorrect or insufficient.
